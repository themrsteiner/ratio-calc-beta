# Texas Childcare Ratio Standards Research

This document summarizes the minimum staff-to-child ratio standards for various Texas childcare facility types, as regulated by Texas Health and Human Services (HHSC).

## 1. Licensed Child-Care Centers (Chapter 746)
Applies to facilities licensed to care for 13 or more children.

| Age of Children | Staff-to-Child Ratio | Maximum Group Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0–11 months | 1:4 | 10 |
| 12–17 months | 1:5 | 13 |
| 18–23 months | 1:9 | 18 |
| 2 years | 1:11 | 22 |
| 3 years | 1:15 | 30 |
| 4 years | 1:18 | 35 |
| 5 years | 1:22 | 35 |
| 6–13 years | 1:26 | 35 |

**Note:** If a group contains children of different ages, the ratio for the *youngest child* in the group applies to the entire group.

## 2. Licensed Child-Care Homes (Chapter 747)
Care for 7 to 12 children (including the provider's own children under 14). Total capacity is 12 children.

*   **1 Caregiver:** Max 12 children, no more than 3 infants (under 18 months) OR 4 infants if at least two are walking.
*   **2 Caregivers:** May care for 12 children of any age mix.

## 3. Registered Child-Care Homes (Chapter 747)
Care for up to 6 children (birth through 13 years) plus 6 additional school-age children (total of 12).

*   **Capacity:** Total 12 children.
*   **Ratios:** No more than 3 children under 18 months at any time.

## 4. Listed Family Homes (Chapter 742)
Care for 1 to 3 unrelated children in the provider's home.

*   **Capacity:** Total number of children (related and unrelated) cannot exceed 12.
*   **Ratios:** No specific staff-to-child ratios, but must maintain safe supervision.

## 5. School-Age and Before/After-School Programs (Chapter 744)
Serve children 5 years or older.

*   **5-year-olds:** 1:22 (Max 35)
*   **6–13 years:** 1:26 (Max 35)
